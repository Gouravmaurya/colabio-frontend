# Cinematic motion: direct the whole journey

Read for motion-led/cinematic work, a missing-animation complaint or an expressive redesign. Use [cinematic-recipes.md](cinematic-recipes.md) for content-led story engines and [motion-recipes.md](motion-recipes.md) for runtime patterns. Cinematic feeling comes from framing, continuity, rhythm and meaningful change; importing GSAP or adding smooth scrolling does not create it.

## Choose ambition from the brief

- **Functional:** immediate feedback, selection, disclosure and layout changes that explain state.
- **Expressive:** coordinated component entrances, purposeful transitions and a recognizable motion language.
- **Cinematic:** a continuing subject transforms across scenes, with authored framing, an intelligible middle state and a deliberate hand-off.

These are scope choices, not a quality ladder. Reading and operational surfaces may need stillness or density. A motion-rich request needs visible choreography across the requested journey, not only a hero zoom and static body. Preserve approved regions. Do not force darkness, full-bleed imagery, loaders, pins, 3D or an effect quota.

## Compose before tweening

Art and motion jointly choose the actual focal asset, crop bounds, title baseline, foreground/midground/background and persistent anchor. Describe how attention travels and which new fact movement reveals. If an image lacks safe crop space, change framing or asset; stretching a tween cannot fix it.

| Family | Useful when it communicates |
| --- | --- |
| Framing, occlusion, masks, match cuts | Whole-to-detail, new context or continuity through a crop |
| Shared objects and layout transitions | The same document/product moves into a new role |
| Layered depth, restrained parallax | Spatial relationships, without unreadable moving copy |
| Type choreography and masked lines | A short title establishes hierarchy; body copy gets a stable hold |
| Ordered collection sequencing | Meaningful order or case narrative, with direct navigation |
| Process/data transformation | Real inputs change inspectable results and explanatory facts |
| Component reveals and assembly | A list, itinerary, comparison or ticket appears in reading order |
| State changes and direct manipulation | Selection, expansion, comparison, drag or feedback with keyboard equivalents |
| Bounded ambient footage/canvas/shaders | Relevant atmosphere, with pause and poster states |

Choose related techniques as one grammar. Headings may share a reveal while images, collections and controls have distinct roles. Avoid uniform fade-up on every block. Deliberate stillness provides reading time and contrast. Tiny zooms, changing CSS values and animation counts do not prove perceptible storytelling.

## Shared scene/state contract

Add these details to the governing scene table for consequential moving scenes. Keep short component reveals proportionate; do not create a second giant specification.

- **Intent:** visitor question, truthful proof and why movement clarifies it.
- **Framing:** real asset/source, crop limits, layers, persistent object/title anchor, selectors or component names.
- **States:** concrete before/middle/settled geometry and visible information. Describe establish → enter → readable hold → transform → resolve where applicable; middle states remain composed.
- **Input:** time, scroll interval, intersection, click, focus or direct manipulation; justify pin length, reversibility and interruption behavior. State how the next scene inherits subject or attention.
- **Ownership:** one runtime owner per animated property, scoped cleanup and resize/font/image refresh. Reflow must not preserve stale progress or hidden content.
- **Phone:** authored touch composition with native scroll, appropriate entrances/transitions and reachable controls. Disabling Lenis does not disable native scroll animation.
- **Reduced/static:** separate stable, complete states for reduced motion, script/library failure and no script. Essential content cannot depend on completing choreography.
- **Evidence:** exact scroll/control path and expected visible states to capture or play back; owner and peer reviewer.

Focus, anchors, direct navigation and search-linked sections must reveal a usable settled target. Controls remain available during motion. Prevent translated children from causing horizontal overflow. Breakpoint/preference rebuilds must restore coherent states without global trigger destruction.

## Review in motion

Inspect an affected body/proof scene and hand-off before scaling out. Play the actual journey on desktop and phone; capture start/middle/settled frames and observe timing, reading holds and continuity. Exercise reverse scroll, rapid navigation, focus into animated regions, resize, runtime reduced-motion changes and blocked libraries. Check clipping, split-text accessible names, touch scrolling, reachable actions and console errors.

An automated transform assertion proves a property changed. A still screenshot proves one frame. Neither alone proves choreography, rhythm or cinematic feeling. Pair engineering checks with observed playback and reasoned peer review against the agreed plan. Record limitations plainly.
