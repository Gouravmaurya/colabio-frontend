From a lump to a vase.

HeatRoom temp.
  1. 01 · Wedge

    Knead the air out.

    Clay is wedged by hand until it’s even all the way through. Trapped air would burst in the kiln.

  2. 02 · Throw

    Centre it, then lift.

    On the wheel the lump is centred, opened and pulled up into a wall. A thrown piece shows faint rings where the fingers climbed.

  3. 03 · Trim

    Dry, then turn the foot.

    Once leather-hard the piece goes back on the wheel and the base is trimmed. Then it dries completely and goes into a first, lower firing.

  4. 04 · Fire

    Glaze, then the long burn.

    The glaze goes on as a chalky slurry and turns to glass in the second firing, at about 1,240°C for stoneware. Typical figures; each kiln differs.
