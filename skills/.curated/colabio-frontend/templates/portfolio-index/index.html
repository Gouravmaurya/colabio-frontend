How a motion brief becomes a system.

  1. Name the change

    One sentence on what the interface must make obvious. If it cannot be said in a line, it cannot be animated in a second.

  2. Prototype the timing

    Rough curves in the real product within two days, because timing is judged by feel, not by mockups.

  3. Write the rules

    Durations, easings and distances become tokens, with a reduced-motion version of every pattern.

  4. Hand over and measure

    Specs, code and a short review a month after launch against the one measurable change.
