# Existing-site comparison gate

Read this before a substantial redesign of an existing product and again before delivery. For a small fix, compare only the affected surface. Respect the user's research constraints and authorized scope.

## Establish the actual baseline

Inspect the rendered incumbent at representative desktop and narrow widths where available. Read its content and inspect its primary journey and distinctive components. Capture comparable views or record an explicit access limitation. Source code, a text scrape, and a screenshot of only the header do not establish a full visual baseline.

Record what the incumbent already does well: product identity, primary action, hierarchy, component anatomy, content/proof, interaction, visual rhythm, responsive behavior, and motion. Distinguish visible marketing claims from verified product capabilities. Never expose private account details in published evidence.

Keep the product's category and value proposition recognizable. A new palette or editorial voice does not authorize turning an AI platform into a property brochure, a shop into a portfolio, or a working tool into a decorative concept. User-approved changes to identity take precedence.

## Define the improvement before building

Choose three to five concrete advances over the baseline, tied to the visitor's task. Examples: show a traceable source-to-result flow, make scenario consequences manipulable, explain uncertainty beside the relevant output, or make a dense comparison usable on mobile. Identify baseline strengths to preserve. Do not impose these example components on unrelated products.

For an ambitious flagship brief, explore one bold product-specific visual mechanism and give it working states. Vary composition and pacing around the product story. Typography, assets, spatial hierarchy, and interaction must carry the idea with motion disabled. Do not use generic gradients, repeated cards, or a cinematic reveal as substitutes for product depth.

## Compare the rendered result

Compare equivalent viewport sizes and the same visitor journey. Record incumbent strength, implemented advance, observed evidence, regressions, and remaining limits for each selected dimension. Include narrow layouts, keyboard/touch alternatives, readable controls, and a reduced-motion route. Correct material regressions before claiming improvement.

A technically valid result may still be visually weaker. Passing tests, completing an evidence schema, loading many skills, and installing GSAP do not prove better design. Do not call a redesign premium, award-level, or better than the incumbent based only on those facts. State specific demonstrated improvements; overall aesthetic superiority remains a judgment, not a certification.

## Stop conditions and delivery

If baseline access fails, disclose that comparison is incomplete and use available project evidence without inventing observations. If a requested improvement exceeds the available content or backend, build an explicitly labeled local sample rather than fake live signals, scores, testimonials, or AI execution. Report what is implemented, simulated, and unverified.

Save the comparison in the project's design/evidence output, not in this skill. Keep original production files and other applications outside the authorized redesign untouched.
