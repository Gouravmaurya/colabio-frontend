# Local mechanism analyses

These are original Colabio design analyses, licensed under the adjacent
[MIT license](mechanism-analyses-license.txt). They are available offline. They
do not reproduce another brand's DESIGN.md, assets, or proprietary identity.
The object-store observations come from the isolated LAYR experiment; the other
two are explicit design hypotheses for the evaluation briefs, not observed client
results. Use them to reason about mechanisms, never as mandatory page skeletons.

## Configurable objects: keep the consequences together

The visitor is choosing among related variants. The useful relationship is
selection to visible object, specifications, price, and bag state. A focal viewer
earns its space when those consequences stay understandable together. A selector
adapted from overlapping source components can feel physical, but native buttons,
visible selected state, and a simple keyboard route must carry the functionality.

In the LAYR browser retest, changing shape/shade updated the preview and price;
the sample bag retained exact variants and consistent totals. A continuous shelf
provided comparison and a separate contour study explained the selected form.
Review also caught title/control overlap and tap-target issues: a compelling idea
did not replace breakpoint inspection. The initial bundle was about 325 KB gzip;
this suggests investigating deferred 3D loading, not claiming a loading-time result.

Transfer the linked decision model and adjacent proof. Do not transfer the clay
palette, vessel imagery, giant typography, or shelf metaphor to unrelated products.

## Professional services: make scope and evidence inspectable

Design hypothesis: a visitor must distinguish services, trust the reasoning, and
know what happens next. Connect each engagement to its inputs, deliverables,
constraints, and a representative process or permitted work sample. A comparison
or navigable case investigation may explain expertise more clearly than repeating
three generic benefit cards. Evidence must come from supplied facts, not invented
client logos or claims.

Typography should support reading and decision-making. Quiet precision, editorial
character, or a more expressive presentation can all work; choose using the actual
audience and assets. Keep inquiry validation and submission boundaries explicit.
This mechanism still needs a rendered evaluation before it can be reported tested.

## Working apps: distinguish state from decoration

Design hypothesis: the user must scan records, act, and recover. The signature
component can be a linked queue/detail/status model rather than a spectacle.
Preserve context during filtering and state changes. Use coherent seeded data,
visible selection, keyboard navigation, clear empty/error states, and undo or
recovery where the actual task requires it. Dense data should not be forced into
large marketing cards to appear premium.

Use restrained motion to indicate continuity and completion. Give narrow screens
a deliberate navigation/detail composition rather than merely squeezing columns.
Evaluate actual task completion and recovery before claiming usability. This is
a proposed mechanism, not a measured app benchmark.
