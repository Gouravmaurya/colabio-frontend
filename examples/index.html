03 / THE OPEN QUESTIONS

leaves questions.

This is where a scenario meets due diligence. The example is not a risk assessment or a prediction of a property’s performance.

01Is the rent assumption supported?+

Compare local, recent rental evidence and verify the property’s rentable condition. This demo’s rent is fictional, not a market estimate.

02What does the simplified model leave out?+

Verify closing costs, management, HOA fees, utilities, capital work, local taxes, and legal requirements. A fixed maintenance allowance cannot establish a building’s condition.

03Which facts still need verification?+

Review title, permits, inspection findings, insurance availability, and local hazards with suitable sources and qualified professionals. No property records are queried in this standalone demo.
